# Behaviors

- Scramble-in text resolves rapidly on brand, navigation, eyebrow, title and metadata.
- Sections reveal with blur, translate and opacity as they enter the article viewport.
- Cards lean subtly toward the pointer and return with spring-like easing.
- The ambient field responds to pointer position and respects reduced-motion settings.
- Theme switch updates the light/dark palette without reloading.
- The lower coordinate control returns the article container to the top.
- The live clock is always rendered in Europe/Istanbul time.
- Contact and proposal forms preserve their route-specific fields and validation.
