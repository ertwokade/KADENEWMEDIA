# Page topology

## Shared shell

- Fixed, full-viewport HUD above all routes.
- Brand at top-left; primary route controls at top-right.
- Live GMT+3 time, pointer coordinates and city at the lower edge.
- A separate full-height, independently scrolling article layer.
- A low-contrast animated field behind the content.

## Inner route structure

1. Editorial page header with mono eyebrow and large TikTok Sans title.
2. Route-specific sections, cards, lists, images or forms.
3. Haoqi-style metadata panel with date, location, studio and route links.

The native home page remains its own immersive WebGL composition. Inner pages use the original Haoqi editorial/MDX topology rather than duplicating the homepage scene.
